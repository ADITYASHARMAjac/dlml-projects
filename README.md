3. My recommended progression
Considering your ML learning direction, I would follow this sequence:
Your ML project roadmap
1. Stage 1 — Classical ML
   
   Projects 1, 3, 5 and 6.
   
   Master preprocessing, pipelines, validation, feature engineering and ensemble learning.
2. Stage 2 — Unsupervised ML and NLP
   
   Projects 7, 10, 11 and 13.
   
   Learn clustering, dimensionality reduction and text representation.
3. Stage 3 — Deep Learning
   
   Projects 14, 15, 17 and 18.
   
   Learn PyTorch, CNNs, backpropagation and neural network optimization.
4. Stage 4 — Transformers and Generative AI
   
   Projects 16, 22, 29 and 31.
   
   Understand attention, tokenization, fine-tuning, LoRA and diffusion.
5. Stage 5 — Research and Systems
   
   Projects 21, 24, 26, 27 and 28.
   
   Study advanced RL, recommendation systems, model monitoring and production architecture.
